# Code of conduct

This project is open to everyone who wants to help. To keep it a good place:

- Be kind and patient. Criticise code and ideas, never people.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks, in issues, pull requests, reviews or anywhere else people meet through this project.
- No sharing of other people's private information (names, accounts, addresses, in-game identities tied to real life) without their say-so.
- Assume good faith. Ask before you assume.
- Keep to the topic: Wuber, the addon, its tests and its docs.

## Enforcement
The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close or lock issues and pull requests, and block people who break these rules. To report someone's behaviour, use the private report form in the **Security** tab and start the title with "Conduct:". The maintainer aims to answer a report within 7 days. The maintainer reads reports privately and does not share who reported.

This text is the maintainer's own short version of the usual community rules; it is not a copy of another project's.
