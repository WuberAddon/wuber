## For players

### Simple and Advanced mode
- **First start:** Wuber now starts in **Simple** mode, which is phone-sized. Riders see the map, the drivers, and one gold main button; drivers see the big button and their queue. Existing users see a one-time notice: `"Wuber is simpler now. Everything else is in Settings: Advanced mode."`
- **Switching modes:** `Settings > This window > "Advanced mode"` (`"Every setting, and Wuber's extra tools."`) or `/wuber advanced` switches between Simple and Advanced. Switching changes nothing you have set, ends no ride, and closes no driver.
- **What Simple leaves out for riders:** The right-click menu on a driver, the `"3 open · 4 online"` count, bounties and the unfolded two-pane World map, the Fly or summon dial (replaced by one line, e.g. `"Summon 30 sec. Fly about 9 min."`), `"About this driver"`, the live portal line, `"Share"`, the Custom tip key (unless a custom tip is already set), and on Trips the habit chips, milestones, and Azeroth Wrapped (earned milestones still pop up).
- **What Simple leaves out for drivers:** `"Skip"` beside the big button, the Sell / Raid bar (kept when you are in a raid), the `"riders want"` line, `Messages` and `Customers` in `"Your business"` (`Blocked` shows when someone is blocked, `Crew` when you have one), row chips, six of the row menu's choices, `"Also called"` and `"Name riders see"`, Earnings' charts, and the shift card's extra tiles. A lone Warlock goes straight to the Drive console.
- **The Drive tab:** Shown to Warlocks, anyone in a crew, and anyone with a crew offer waiting.
- **Empty Ride screen:** The OpenMap line sits under `"Why?"` when there is space for it.

### A cleaner look, new typeface, and moving pages
- **Flat cards:** Coloured glows, halos, shines, card shadows, and gradients (including on `"Your usual"` and the `"you"` dot) are gone. Cards share one flat colour and one corner shape.
- **Colours:** **Gold** (a warm yellow) is for prices and for the one main button of a screen; it is also the colour of the 1,000-summon pin ring and the Founder badge. **Violet** with dark ink text is for chosen filters, tips, periods, options, switches, stars, routes, and ordinary map rings. **Red** is the single warning colour. The Settings cog is never gold.
- **Buttons and feedback:** Buttons come in three kinds: the gold main button, a grey second button, and a violet text link. Every button, row, card, chip, and switch darkens on mouse-down and plays one soft click.
- **One main button in one place:** Full-width and gold just above the bottom tabs. On the Ride screen it reads `"Ride again · <place> · <price>"`, `"Request a summon"` during raid summons, `"Ping drivers"` when nobody is online, or `"Pin"` while setting a bounty. Confirm, the ride, Done, the Messages editor, a customer's note, and Set up driving use the same spot; Set up driving uses the top-left back arrow instead of a Back button.
- **Tabs at the bottom, cog at the top:** `Ride`, `Trips`, and `Drive` sit at the bottom (the active tab in violet). The old top bar and logo are gone: the map runs to the top with the place on the left and `Wide`/`Narrow`, the Settings cog, and `X` at the top right. Subpages show a back arrow and title at the top without the cog.
- **Live black pill:** During a ride, the black pill at the top shows `"Finding a driver"`, `"Accepted"`, `"Invited"`, `"Summoning"`, `"Portal opening"` / `"Portal open"` with `"<tapped> of <need>"`, and `"Arrived"` with the price. Clicking it opens your ride from any tab or Settings.
- **Tabs hide during a ride:** While a ride is active, the tab bar drops away. Use the top-left back arrow to visit Home with tabs while the ride carries on, and click the black pill to return to the ride.
- **Sliding pages and sheets:** Subpages slide in from the right (0.28 s) and back from the left; tab switches are instant; nothing slides in combat. Raincheck, Report a bug, and login tips rise as full-width sheets from the tab bar (below the top-right buttons so they stay clickable), dimming the page behind them. What's new and the mid-flight offer sit as cards beside the phone.
- **Settings, Sound and effects:** `"Reduce motion"` (`"Pages and sheets fade in place instead of sliding."`) makes pages and sheets fade in place and stops the spinning portal art and pulsing rings.
- **Inter typeface and text sizes:** Text is set in Inter with equal-width digits so prices line up cleanly, using seven named sizes (body text is 14, second lines 12, smallest captions 11). Long place names on the route pill, customer notes, settings rows, login tips, and narrow labels wrap or grow their row instead of truncating, and Wuber's text fields use their own font so they never alter the game's chat box font.
- **Wuber's own tooltips:** Tooltips appear as Wuber's own small card beside the control rather than on top of it. Hovering over any truncated single-line label shows its full text in the tooltip first.

### Careful auto mode and auto-reply whispers
- **Drive, Auto mode:** Wuber auto-invites only when a whisper clearly asks for a ride for the sender. Anything uncertain waits in your list as a clickable lead. Lines with seller words (`"WTS"`, `"whisper me"`, `"dm me"`, etc.) or linked items/spells never auto-invite, and in public chat a bare place name without an asking word is ignored.
- **Settings, Driving:** `"Whisper back to public requests"` (`"Advanced: one whisper, then they whisper you."`, on by default) whispers once when someone in public chat clearly asks for a summon to your place: `'Want a summon to <place>? Whisper me "summon <place>".'` They stay a lead in your list. Wuber whispers at most once an hour per person, never in combat, in a raid, in an instance, or during a ride, and never to group members, other drivers, or blocked players; multiple drivers at the same place take turns. Plain-text notes under `"Invite riders who whisper me"` and `"Whisper back to public requests"` explain what Wuber will not do.
- **Auto-invite record:** The wide Drive side pane shows your session record, such as `"214 auto-invites · 0 wrong"`.
- **Oops, wrong invite:** On an auto-invited rider's row menu (when you lead the group), `"Oops, wrong invite"` (`"Says sorry and removes them"`) queues `"Oops: remove <name>"` on the big button. Your next click whispers sorry and removes them, and remembers that player and those words locally as leads. `Messages` has an editable `"Oops, wrong invite"` line, and `Report a bug` includes `"Wrong invites (last 10):"` with names anonymised.

### Flight masters, flying, and mid-flight offers
- **Flight map price tags:** On the flight master's map, places with an open driver show a three-colour ring marker and a clickable price tag. Clicking the tag opens Wuber on that driver's Confirm screen (`"<driver> · summon to <place> · <wait> sec · costing <price>. Flying: about <mins> min."`), while flight points keep their own clicks. The tags sit on the flight map itself and are placed again when the map is resized, so they stay clickable.
- **Mid-flight summon offer:** Once per flight with at least 90 seconds left and an open driver at your destination, a card offers a faster summon (`"About <mins> min left. A summon takes about <wait> sec."`, `"The summon is at <place>: check your world map to see it."`), with a gold **`"Book · <price>"`** main button and a **`"Don't show"`** link. You can drag the card anywhere (`"Drag to move"`), and that driver's pin is highlighted on the world map while the offer is up.
- **Settings, Riding:** `"Flight offers"` (`"A summon that beats flying: beside the flight map, on it, and while you fly."`) controls all flight-master and mid-flight offers.
- **Skip the bird:** On the flight master's map, the offer card no longer goes away by itself (2.3.2 hid it after 6 seconds). It stays until you close it (the x, `"Don't show again"` or **`"Book"`**), take a flight or close the flight map, and it now shows whenever a summon beats flying by at least a minute (2.3.2 asked for a bigger saving). It shows two short lines (`"Summon <time>. Fly <time>."` and `"<driver> · <place> · <price>"`), a **`"Book"`** button and a `"Don't show again"` link, with the full saving breakdown in its tooltip.
- **Flying estimates and reloads:** A summon taken while flying no longer teaches a falsely short flight time, a flight in progress is kept through a `/reload`, and the default flight-speed estimate for untimed characters is now 30 yards/second (about 6% longer than before).

### Finding a driver, booking, and the ride
- **Ride screen, driver list:** Drivers sit in one card with thin dividers: picture/initial, name, Founder badge, price in gold, heart, and arrow. The `"Options"` button replaces `"..."` on rows. `"Usually about 45 sec"` is appended to the driver's second line when enough timed summons exist, and `"Your usual"` is not offered for the city you are already in. `"No drivers online"` shows `"Usually here <hours>"`, `"Ping drivers"`, `"Tell me"`, `"Pin a bounty"`, and `"Why?"`.
- **Ride screen, Confirm:** Shows `"Favourite"` under the heart and `" · usually <time>"` on the grey line. In Advanced mode, the Fly or summon dial formats sides as `"2 min · costing 23s"` / `"30 sec · costing 50s"` and wraps long lines cleanly. PvP warnings use compact mode when space is tight. Requesting in combat works when the booking card was already open, and an offline or gone driver clears the previous driver's price, route, and dial.
- **Ride screen, Waiting and Portal:** Waiting for a portal reads `"Stay put"` and `"Accept the portal when it pops up."` with just the two-word passphrase. The portal art spins slowly anticlockwise when active (stopping with Reduce motion or Effects off), and the rider's `"you"` dot is hidden on the live ride page. If the game's summon box opens with no summoner name, the Ride page still shows `"<summoner> is summoning you to <place>"`.
- **Ride screen, Waiting in line and group state:** While a driver still has you in line and stays heard, Wuber keeps waiting (giving up only after 150 seconds of silence). Whisper-only requests show their real 120-second bar and receive busy-driver updates and passphrase changes. Auto-accept joins the driver's group even when the `"invited"` message arrives before the game's invite box, and leaving a group after a summon expires steps the ride back to `"accepted"` (`"A group invite is on its way ..."`) instead of leaving it stuck.
- **Ride screen, Paying and Done:** If a stranger opens trade, the pay card dims and warns you (even at price 0) and hides `Share`. Part-payments show remaining `"left of"` owed plus tip, and picking a tip while trade is open updates the trade strip. In combat, `Share` reads `"Share after combat"`. Done shortens the comparison line to `"Flying: <amt> · the summon cost the same"`, omits `"Nothing to pay."` beside `"Ask for a raincheck"`, and shows `"Not paid, so no level progress. Drivers can block riders who don't pay."` on an unpaid end. The star tooltip reads: `"Rate your ride. Your driver is told the stars. You see your ratings in Trips."`
- **Searching and hidden drivers:** Typing a driver's name in `"Where to?"` no longer warns `"No place called ..."`, the search heading wraps clear of `"N open · M online"`, a hidden driver who signs off no longer comes back as online when you press `"Show all"` on the `"Hidden drivers"` row in Settings, and re-booking while a ride or raincheck is active keeps the first ride working.

### Driving, the queue, and the big button
- **Drive page, layout and console:** Every listed rider has a row past the eighth. The Sell / Raid switch sits at the top beside the round buttons, and `"Your business"` (`Crew`, `Messages`, `Blocked`, `Earnings`, `Customers`) sits at the end of the page. Opening a sub-screen hides the Drive console behind it so nothing overlaps, and when a two-line fare needs room on the folded console, gold stacks cleanly above the ride count.
- **Your summon time:** Today's takings shows a grey `"Your summons: about 45 sec"` line, your average from request to arrival, once three summons have been counted; its tooltip says how it was counted.
- **Drive page, Close and Retire:** Clicking `Close` while riders are still listed asks `"1 rider is still with you. Close anyway?"` / `"<N> riders are still with you. Close anyway?"`. The Retire / another-addon card wraps cleanly.
- **Drive page, Queue and summon status:** Riders in your queue are kept across reloads, and a rider who walks up to you or arrives by someone else's summon leaves the list after 4 seconds. Each rider row shows what the game knows about their summon (even without Wuber): `"has the summon window"`, `"summon accepted"`, or `"declined: summon again?"`.
- **Big button and mini-console:** The big button wraps onto two lines when needed, does not cast on a rider it has seen get a summon in this session (`"<Name> has a summon waiting"`), and uses Soul Shard loss to avoid double-casting a summon that went out with two non-Wuber helpers. At login, Warlocks are not told the summoning spell is unknown while the spellbook is still loading, and if the game delays the summoner's name, Wuber retries for a few seconds. The floating mini-console's `"..."` button is now `"Options"`, and when vertical space is tight it trims rows so `Pause`/`Close` stay on screen.
- **Shard counter and Price & place:** The shard gauge reads `"12 shards"` / `"1 shard"`. Price and place wraps matches cleanly and notes `"Leave it empty to use where you stand."` under the place box.
- **The big button says why:** `"<name> is in the group"` with `"they are already here - no summon needed"` when there is nothing to summon. After `"Take off the list"`, a rider who is still in your group gets `"Remove <name>"` on the big button: one click of yours, never automatic.
- **Customers and skips:** Shift-clicking Skip toasts `"<name> is skipped and blocked"` only when actually blocked, otherwise `"<name> is skipped"`. In Advanced mode, `Customers` lets you set `"Don't serve again until <date>"` on a rider who left owing (a 30-day local hold that prevents auto-invite and auto-reply; nothing is sent over the network). Saving a customer note clears any `"Remove note?"` warning.
- **Hidden network resilience:** In a raid or boss fight, Wuber holds hidden addon messages and sends them after, avoiding `"This account can't send addon messages"`. After a loading screen it sends at most 2 at once, then 1 per second; before a hidden whisper to a known player ID it asks the game whether they are online when supported; and if Wuber's hidden channel has a password, the owner clears it or Wuber prints `"The Wuber channel has a password; requests still work by whisper."`. If Wuber cannot join its network after two tries it says `"Couldn't join the Wuber network. You may be in 10 chat channels already. You may see fewer drivers, but requests still work by whisper."`. Same-player whispers under two name spellings are merged into one row in memory.

### Payment by mail and self-sent messages
- **Payment by mail:** For a rider who left owing, the Done screen shows `"Still owe the fare? Send <amount> to <driver> by mail, subject Wuber."`, and the driver's big button offers `"Tell <name>"` for 10 minutes after walk-out to whisper the same. At an open mailbox, Wuber notes matching mail (`"Mail seen: <amount> from <name>, <day>."`) for information only—Wuber never opens, fills, sends, or takes mail, and mail never marks a ride paid automatically.
- **Messages, self-sent whispers:** Three lines can be sent by themselves to your own rider, each **off by default** in `Messages` and never sent in combat, in a raid, in an instance, or to anyone who did not ask you for a ride: `"Send the greeting by itself"` (when you start their summon), `"Send the pay reminder by itself"` (25 seconds after they land), and `"You're next"` / `"Send You're next by itself"` (to a rider without Wuber who is first in line for 10 seconds).
- **Messages editor:** The Messages list shows names and when-sent only, with the preview inside the editor; an emptied box previews the default wording, a crew captain sees `"Saved · your crew uses it too"`, and a refused Save keeps your draft and scrolls into view.

### Crews and raid summons
- **Crews:** Leaving or ending a crew restores your own price, place, largest group, kick, and whisper settings. A full crew says `"Crew is full (8)"`, Fair split counts survive `/reload`, crew messages are held and retried when chat is locked in a boss fight, and a non-Warlock helper leading the group sees `"<who> leads the group: only the leader can invite. Ask <who> to make you leader."`.
- **Crew setup:** `"Send offers again"` resends to everyone who has not answered. Adding by name rejects invalid names (`"That isn't a player's name. Type one word of 2 to 12 letters, with -Realm for another realm."`) and drops offline offers (`"Nobody called <name> is online, so that crew offer is gone."`), while `"Pick your crew"` keeps per-person ticks.
- **Raid summons:** `"here"` means the summoner can see the raider (not just same zone), the `+` on a `"Not here"` raider is dimmed until `Start`, and the narrow empty card wraps `"Raiders type 123 in raid chat, or tap Request a summon"` cleanly. Crew Warlocks pressing Start/Stop see `"Your captain starts and stops the raid summons."`, and the `"Not here"` header stays clear of overlays. A raider who types "nvm", "cancel" or "no summon" is taken off the list, even while summons are paused ("summon pls, no mount" still asks). A Warlock in the crew that runs the raid summons no longer sees a `"Request a summon"` card that does nothing.

### Driver titles, Earnings, and Trips
- **Driver titles:** Drivers earn titles from verified Wuber app rides (counted once per rider per day, starting from zero in 2.4.0; never crew or chat riders): `Learner` (0), `Cabbie` (10), `Black Cab` (50), `Chauffeur` (250), and `Has The Knowledge` (1,000).
- **Where titles show:** As a chip on the driver's list row and in the world-map pin tooltip (with a gold ring at 1,000), and with your own count and progress bar on the wide Drive page. Reaching a new title shows a card once with `"Tell the guild"` to post `"[Wuber] <name> has summoned <n> riders and is now a <title>."` (or `"[Wuber] <name> has summoned <n> riders and Has The Knowledge."` at 1,000) to guild chat (or party/raid without a guild).
- **Founder crown and gold portal:** Founders keep their crown and the gold portal when the game gives no realm name, for a character whose realm the game named at an earlier login (Wuber remembers it for that character).
- **Earnings:** Charts and `"Best hours"` squares use the violet ramp with `"less"` and `"more"` labels, and hours when buyers asked while you were closed appear as outlined violet squares. `"Scroll for more"` hides when Settings is open.
- **Trips:** The `"Scroll for your trips"` pill hides covered rows and hides behind Settings, and `Azeroth Wrapped` omits `"Share"` for a driving-only month.

### World map and pins
- **Pins and "Wuber only":** Open driver pins wear a three-colour ring (violet, gold, teal) over a dark rim that turns when Effects is set to Full. The world map has a `"Wuber only"` pill (`"Dims the rest of the map so Wuber's pins stand out."`), saved between sessions.
- **Colours and labels:** Routes, arrows, and ordinary rings are violet (gold is for prices and the 1,000-summon ring). Your current continent is tinted brighter with its name in violet, sea routes split with `"continues by <boat/zeppelin>"` (with Horde `"Boat or zeppelin"` in the legend), `"Wanted: <place>"` / `"1 rider wants to go here."` appear on bounty coins, and the driver `"You"` tag and sea route labels stay clear of continent names, pins, and the rider dot.
- **Safe map opener:** When Wuber opens the world map for you (for example on a driver), it asks the game to do it in the game's own safe way. It never does it in a fight: it says `"Leave combat first, then open the map."` instead.

### Settings, Help, and Bug reports
- **Shorter, grouped Settings:** Settings is shorter: Simple mode shows only the rows most players need, and the rest wait behind **Advanced mode**. The first page has `Advanced mode` at the top, section rows, `"Most used"` switches (`Drivers on the world map`, `Sounds`, plus `Auto-accept my driver's invite` when Advanced mode is on), and `This window` / `Help` / `Report a bug`.
- **Merged and moved rows:** Related controls are grouped into single rows: `Tips` (with `Replay`), `Flight offers`, `Group size` (`"One at a time"` fits cleanly), `Chat channels`, `Riders who leave without paying` (`"Block and tell them"`, `"Block quietly"`, `"Do nothing"`), `Big button on screen`, `Big button size` (with `Fit`), `Lock the big button` (with `Reset place`), `Alert character` (with `Test` and `Forget`), `Badges over heads`, `Window size`, and `Wide pages` (with `Always narrow` inside it). `My target per hour` moved to `Earnings`, `Fair split` moved to `Crew`, `Where to get Wuber` moved to the end of `Help`, and `Badges over heads` + the nameplates button moved to `Sound and effects`.
- **Resets with Undo:** Resetting window position, wide/narrow per page, big button place, or Earnings stats shows an `"Undo"` button for 10 seconds.
- **Settings, Riding:** `"What Wuber sends"` states Wuber's sending limits in plain words (with the full explanation in its tooltip).
- **Search and Game Options:** Searching in Simple mode still finds Advanced settings, marks them `"Advanced"`, and points to the switch. Clicking `X` when Settings was opened over the game's Options closes Settings back to where you came from without hiding Wuber (the game's Options is left as it is); a second `X` hides Wuber.
- **Login tips, What's new, and self-opening cards:** Login tips, the first-open guide (`Tip 1 of 5`, now solid), Skip the bird, and `"What's new in Wuber 2.4"` share one rule: never in combat, never in a cinematic, never in the first seconds after login, and one at a time. Cards closed by the game come back later, and opening `Trips` right after first-opening `Drive` stays on `Trips`. Toasts stay clear of the chat frame, HUD, and What's new card.
- **What's new, once:** After an update, a `"What's new in Wuber 2.4"` card shows once, a few seconds after you log in (once per account; never on a fresh install, in combat, on a ride, on a flight or while summoning). Closing it counts as seen; if the game hides it first, it comes back later.
- **Login tips:** One short tip the first time you open Wuber after you log in (not after a `/reload`), each tip once, with `"Show me"`, `"Next tip"` and `"Don't show again"`. Settings has a `"Tips"` switch (with `Replay`), and `/wuber tips` brings the last one back.
- **Help and Bug reports:** Help and Settings keep separate scrollbars, and driving options opened from the Drive tab gear show to anyone. `Report a bug` closes on `Escape`, preserves `"unknown"`, anonymises realm tails, and lists the last 5 blocked actions and the last 10 wrong invites.

### Key bindings, AddOns page, and chat commands
- **Game Key Bindings and Options:** The game's Key Bindings list has a `"Wuber"` section with `"Open or close Wuber"` and `"Key for the big button"`. `Options > AddOns > Wuber` has an `"Open Wuber settings"` button.
- **New and updated chat commands:**
  - `/wuber advanced` — switches between Simple and Advanced mode.
  - `/wuber why` — lists what Wuber is hiding right now and why.
  - `/wuber tips` / `/wuber tips off` — shows, enables, or disables login tips.
  - `/wuber updates on` / `off` — turns update notices on or off.
  - `/wuber drive accept|public|autotrade|remind|shards|away|wants|sharenotes on|off` — toggles the eight drive switches removed from the Settings UI.
  - `/wuber drive bind escape` — clears the big button's key (`"Escape can't be the big button's key. The key was removed."`).
  - `/wuber price` — caps the maximum fare at `9,999g 99s 99c`.
- **Minimap button:** `"Shift-right-click: pause or resume driving."` works while open, and the button pulses cleanly.

## Not yet tried in the real game

These are built and pass their automatic tests, but nobody has seen them in the real game yet, so please do not count on them:
- The whisper back to a public request (Settings, Driving, `"Whisper back to public requests"`), and the mail note at an open mailbox (`"Mail seen: <amount> from <name>, <day>."`).
- Dragging the mid-flight card (`"Drag to move"`) and its saved place.
- Founder crowns and the gold portal on a character that has never logged in while the game gave its realm name: Wuber can only remember a realm from a login where the game named it.
