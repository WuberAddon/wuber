Wuber earnings heatmap demo data

When to summon, when to level

Green: hours that paid at least your target, with 20+ minutes open. Blue: hours you have barely been open, but over an hour or more of watching, buyers asked for your spot at least twice an hour. Everything else: level.

Gold per open hour, by weekday and hour

Your whole crew together, over the time you were open (open and not paused). Hover a square for the details.

Buyers asking, by weekday and hour

People asking for a summon in public chat, per hour the game was running, open or not. This shows demand at times you have not tried yet.

Wuber requests, by weekday and hour

Ride requests that reached you through the Wuber app, per hour the game was running. Declined: closed, paused, another place, a price riders were not shown, or a blocked player.

Rival drivers online, by weekday and hour

Other Wuber drivers (not your crew), the most seen at once in each hour, averaged over the days logged. Fewer rivals and many buyers: a good time to be open.

Day by day

Gold each hour on each day. Grey: open but no rides. Striped: not open.
